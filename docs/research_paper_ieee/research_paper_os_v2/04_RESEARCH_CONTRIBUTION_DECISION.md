## Main empirical finding

> Statistical unusualness and polarization-like modulation evidence are related but are not equivalent within the project archive.

**Current strength:** **MODERATE-HIGH AS A BOUNDED DESCRIPTIVE FINDING**. Agents 4 and 6 support it through discordant cases, including Sco X-1, Her X-1, Crab P01_0005, and anomalous blank-sky observations. It is not a universal claim, a polarization result, or evidence that either branch validates the other.

## Agent-8/Agent-11 final provisional hierarchy

**Primary — Moderate:** A traceable, product-aware XAI framework for archive-relative screening of heterogeneous POLIX Level-2 observations.

**Secondary — Moderate:**

1. A 15-feature observation representation preserving POLIX product-family provenance.
2. A deterministic, project-specific, model-informed local feature-ranking method.
3. A scientifically separate WeightedRoll and empirical blank-sky harmonic diagnostic branch that avoids circular confirmation.

**Supporting evidence/implementation, not standalone contributions:** seed stability, contamination sensitivity, jackknife influence analysis, feature-tier comparison, six-case neutralization sanity check, and Flask interface.

**Rejected as contributions:** algorithmic novelty, SHAP/LIME method, general faithfulness, benchmark superiority, calibrated polarimetry, discovery, confirmed anomalies, or future-release generalization.

This hierarchy is used in the Draft-2 guide candidate but remains subject to faculty approval.

## Provisional five-contribution framing for guide discussion

1. Product-aware, interpretable observation representation from several POLIX Level-2 product families.
2. Archive-relative unsupervised candidate screening with complementary pipeline components.
3. A deterministic, project-specific, model-informed local feature-ranking heuristic with deterministic product-family grouping.
4. A limited six-case, top-three-to-zero perturbation sanity check, explicitly not a general faithfulness validation.
5. A separate empirical WeightedRoll/blank-sky branch that avoids circular confirmation and demonstrates archive-bounded non-equivalence, with harmonic coefficients and fit-quality caveats retained in the main paper and PD proxies supplementary at most.

No item is approved for Draft 2 until Agent 8 and the guide review the framing. Agent 5's audit specifically rejects unqualified “model-specific feature attribution,” formal faithfulness, SHAP-equivalence, causal explanation, and cross-observation interpretation of combined scores.
