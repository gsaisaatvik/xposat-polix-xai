- Feature semantics remain engineering descriptions until POLIX-aware domain review confirms stronger terminology.
- The exact historical website artifact that produced the entropy-first Sco X-1 narrative remains unavailable.
- No prospective observation set, trusted anomaly label set, or expert candidate adjudication exists.

None of these items invalidates the bounded archive-screening result. They prevent stronger validity, calibration, and generalization claims.
